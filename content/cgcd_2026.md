# Conspicuous Gas, Cryptic Dust

The movies below show TESS light curves for the complex periodic
variables in Bouma et al. (2026), _Conspicuous Gas, Cryptic Dust:
Spectroscopy and Chromaticities of Complex Periodic Variables_.

The movies span 2018-2025 (sectors 1 through 96); their coverage is
dictated by the TESS scanning law.  The light curves are folded to the
periods and reference times noted in each movie's title.  The TESS
full-frame image cadence improved over the mission, which is why the
sampling for TIC 300651846 and TIC 262400835 improves in 2020 and 2022.

Production notes:

_Light curve sources_: TIC 300651846: TARS (Boyle et al. 2026).  LP
12-502 and DG CVn: SPOC (Jenkins et al. 2016).  TIC 262400835: QLP
(Huang et al. 2020).

_Cleaning_: For DG CVn, I subtracted a 2.6 hour sinusoid at the
secondary period from the raw light curve.  For TIC 262400835, I
subtracted a 12.7 hour sinusoid, and a 4.31 day sinusoid, for the
secondary and tertiary signals in the TESS light curve.
