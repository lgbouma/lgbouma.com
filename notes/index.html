Rotation Periods Across The Sky!

2026.03.05. The TESS All Sky Rotation Survey (TARS), led by Andy Boyle, is out! This work leverages TESS images from 2018-2025 to search for photometric rotation periods of stars within 500 pc. Check out the paper and the data viz webpage.
